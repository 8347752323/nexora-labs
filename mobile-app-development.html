Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to key questions about building your mobile application with Nexora Labs.

How long does it take to develop a mobile app?

A typical MVP or focused mobile app takes 6 to 10 weeks from discovery to store launch. Larger enterprise apps with extensive custom backends or hardware integrations typically range from 12 to 16 weeks.

How much does a mobile app cost?

Pricing is determined by the app's scope, supported platforms (iOS, Android, or both), and backend complexity. We provide clear, transparent fixed-price milestone contracts with zero hidden fees.

Do you publish apps on Google Play Store and Apple App Store?

Yes. We handle the entire release lifecycle—including app store guidelines compliance, screenshot assets, metadata preparation, privacy manifests, and review approval.

Do you provide maintenance and support?

Yes. We provide flexible post-launch SLA support packages covering OS updates (iOS/Android releases), security patches, third-party API updates, and analytics monitoring.
