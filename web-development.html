Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Nexora Labs.

What is the typical project timeline?

Most business websites and landing pages take 2 to 4 weeks. Complex custom web applications typically range between 6 to 10 weeks depending on custom feature requirements and integrations.

How much does a web development project cost?

Cost depends on complexity, features, and technical scope. We provide clear, fixed-scope proposals tailored to your budget with transparent milestones and zero hidden fees.

Do you provide support and maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer continuous support packages covering security updates, server monitoring, bug fixes, and feature enhancements to keep your application running smoothly.

Will the website work seamlessly on all mobile devices?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first, ensuring smooth performance and pixel-perfect responsiveness across iPhones, Android devices, tablets, and desktops.
